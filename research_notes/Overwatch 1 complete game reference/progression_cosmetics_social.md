# Overwatch 1 player-facing meta systems (final OW1 state, 2022): progression, cosmetics, currencies, events, competitive, social, career profile

Source note: overwatch.fandom.com and overwatch.wiki.gg returned 402/401/403 to automated fetches during this research. Fan-wiki facts below come from the Weird Gloop Overwatch Wiki (overwatch.weirdgloop.org, raw wikitext fetched 2026-10-15). It is now the main community wiki and keeps OW1 "history" subsections. Primary Blizzard sources (official patch-note archive, Blizzard forums) are used wherever they were reachable. Fan-wiki claims are labeled as such.

## Player level and XP

### Takeaway
At launch, OW1 XP came from time in match (~4 XP/s), match completion (250), a win (500), a medal (≤150), a daily first-match/win bonus (1,500) and a +20% group bonus. Each level-up granted one Loot Box. The Nov 15, 2016 patch made every level after 100 cost a flat 20,000 XP. Levels 2–13 stayed the same and levels 14–100 got "slightly" cheaper (Blizzard gave no exact table). Borders changed every 10 levels and each 100-level "promotion" added a star. Border tiers were Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond, and the frame art stopped changing around level 2,901. Endorsement levels gave Loot Boxes, not XP, in OW1.

### Cited Findings
- XP sources (OW1, launch-era fan wiki): time in match **4.01 XP/second**; Bronze medal 50 XP, Silver 100 XP, Gold 150 XP (only the highest medal counts, max 150 bonus); **Match Finish 250 XP**; **Match Won 500 XP**; **First Match of the Day 1,500 XP**; **Group Bonus +20% XP**. — [Fextralife Progression System](https://overwatch.wiki.fextralife.com/Progression_System)
- Conflict on the daily bonus: Dot Esports calls it "first win of the day", 1,500 XP. It also gives 500 XP for a win, 150 XP for a match finish (not 250) and +20% for a group. Fextralife calls it "First Match of the Day". — [Dot Esports (via search snippet)](https://dotesports.com/news/overwatch-experience-explained); [Fextralife](https://overwatch.wiki.fextralife.com/Progression_System)
- Launch-era XP needed to reach the next level (fan wiki table): L1 1,500; L2 3,000; L3 4,500; L4 6,000; L5 7,500; L6 8,500; L7 9,500; L8 10,500; L9 11,500; L10 12,500; L11 13,500; L12 14,500; L13 15,500; L14 16,500; L15 17,500; L16 18,500; L17 19,000; L18 19,500; L19+ 20,000. — [Fextralife](https://overwatch.wiki.fextralife.com/Progression_System) (some community sources say the curve flattens after level 22; [search summary of GameFAQs/others](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/182930-overwatch-2016/76484003))
- Official Nov 15, 2016 patch: "The amount of experience needed to reach levels 2 through 13 remains unchanged"; "levels 14 through 100 has been slightly reduced"; "levels after 100 is now constant (20,000 XP)". The developer comment says the curve used to reset after each promotion, so the first 23 levels of each tier (1-23, 101-123, 201-223) went fast; post-promotion levels "will no longer reset". — [Blizzard patch notes, Nov 2016](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2016/11/)
- One Loot Box per level-up; each box holds four items (cosmetics or credits). — [Fextralife](https://overwatch.wiki.fextralife.com/Progression_System); "In Overwatch 1, one Loot Box was received for every level gained." — [Weird Gloop wiki: Loot Box](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Loot_Box)
- Borders: portrait borders change every 10 levels; a star is added every 100 levels ("promotion"). Bronze runs levels 1–600 and Silver 601–1,200. Gold starts at level 1,201, where the five silver stars are removed. Platinum covers 1,801–2,400 and Diamond starts at 2,401, with stars then alternating diamond/gold. "The portrait border stops changing at level 2901, though, players can continue leveling." — [dbltap](https://www.dbltap.com/posts/6243926-overwatch-level-system-how-does-it-work); [GameDaft](https://gamedaft.com/overwatch-level-borders-explained/); [Fextralife](https://overwatch.wiki.fextralife.com/Progression_System)
- A player reported reaching "MAX border level 3000" (~3,003 hours). — [Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/im-now-max-border-level-3000/652791)
- Fextralife says there is no level cap. — [Fextralife](https://overwatch.wiki.fextralife.com/Progression_System)
- Public career profiles exposed `level` and `prestige` as separate fields (example: level "42", prestige "8"), plus `borderURL` and `starsURL`. In the final OW1 profile, the displayed level was a 1–100 value plus a prestige count. — [overwatch-stats-api README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/overwatch-stats-api@2.1.0/README.md)
- Competitive Play unlocked at **player level 25**. This was still true in Season 35 (2022). — [Blizzard forum "Welcome to Season 35"](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/welcome-to-season-35-of-competitive-play/680081/1)
- OW2 change: OW1 levels and loot-box progression were replaced by the Battle Pass. Endorsements grant Battle Pass XP in OW2, where in OW1 they granted Loot Boxes. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Endorsements](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Endorsements)

### Inferences
- Server model: store total XP and derive `level = 1..100 within prestige` plus `prestige = floor((L-1)/100)`. The border is `((L-1) mod 100) / 10`-ish within the tier art set, and the star count is `prestige mod 6` with tier changes every 6 promotions (600 levels). After Nov 2016, levels >100 cost 20,000 XP. The exact 2016–2022 values for levels 14–100 are unknown. The launch-era table is a reasonable approximation but slightly overstates them.
- The client probably computes borders and stars from asset data (portrait-frame unlocks; OWLib has an `UnlockType.PortraitFrame`). The server likely only needs to send the level/XP numbers. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- Exact post-Nov-2016 XP table for levels 14–100 was not found (Blizzard only says "slightly reduced").
- Any later XP-formula changes (e.g., consecutive-match bonus, leaver XP penalty, Arcade/Custom XP rules, XP-boost events such as double XP weekends) between 2017 and 2022 could not be confirmed from accessible sources.
- Whether the 1,500 bonus needed a win or just a completed match in the final 2022 build is unresolved (sources conflict).
- I found no source confirming any endorsement XP bonus in OW1. The "50 XP per endorsement given" claim could not be verified.

## Loot boxes, credits and currencies

### Takeaway
Each OW1 Loot Box held 4 items with at least one Rare or better. Per Blizzard's 2017 China disclosure, an Epic came every ~5.5 boxes and a Legendary every ~13.5 boxes on average. Duplicates paid credits (5/15/50/200 by rarity), and boxes could also contain credits directly (50/150–200/500). Credit prices were 25/75/250/1,000, and new event items cost 3× (75/225/750/3,000). Boxes came from level-ups, Arcade wins (3/week), endorsement-level rewards, events and purchase. Paid boxes were removed in Belgium (2018) and all box sales ended August 30, 2022. OW2 auto-opened leftover boxes.

Base boxes could drop any non-event skin. Seasonal boxes dropped only current-event skins. Anniversary boxes could drop any past event skin. OW1 had 363 loot-box-obtainable skins. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Loot Box](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Loot_Box); [Weird Gloop wiki: Skins](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Skins)

- Arcade weekly boxes, Game Informer at the Nov 2016 Arcade launch: "winning three matches of any game mode in the current Arcade rotation will net players an additional loot box", up to three per week. The fan wiki says boxes come on the 3rd, 6th and 9th Arcade win per weekly reset. The sources conflict on whether 3 or 9 wins are needed for 3 boxes. — [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2016/11/04/how-overwatch-39-s-new-arcade-mode-works.aspx); [search summary of Overwatch Wiki / Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/overwatch/whats-overwatch-arcade-mode-today-309751/). ### Inferences
- A server reproduction should keep separate balances for Credits, Competitive Points and OWL Tokens. In OWLib terms these are `Currency`, `CompetitiveCurrency` and `OWLToken` unlock/currency kinds, and the paid "VirtualCurrency" did not exist in OW1. Loot boxes should be held as per-type counts (standard + event types via `LootBoxType`).
- Recommended RNG model for a preservation server: 4 items; force ≥1 Rare+; P(box has Epic) ≈ 0.185, P(box has Legendary) ≈ 0.075. Fill the rest with Commons and Rares, using a credit-pile item (50/150/500) as a possible item. Re-roll owned items when unowned items exist, otherwise pay duplicate credits (5/15/50/200). The exact OW1 duplicate-protection algorithm is undocumented. Treat this as an approximation.
- Event pricing rule for the gallery: price = base_cost × 3 if the item is from the currently running event and first released this year; otherwise base cost.

### Gaps
- The exact duplicate-protection mechanics after the 2017 "reduced duplicates" change (and its patch date) were not retrieved.
- Exact Arcade weekly rule in the 2022 build (3 wins/box vs 3rd/6th/9th win) is unresolved.
- I could not confirm the Netherlands status or whether China had different box rules (e.g., credit-bundle sales with boxes as "gifts").
- I found no official endorsement-reward cadence or amounts (only forum reports).

## Cosmetic types, default unlocks and the Hero Gallery

### Takeaway
OW1 per-hero cosmetics were Skins (Common/Rare/Epic/Legendary), Emotes, Victory Poses, Voice Lines, Sprays, Highlight Intros and Weapons (golden). Account-wide cosmetics were Player Icons and portrait frames/levels. Each hero started with Classic skin(s), a Heroic intro, a Heroic voice line, a Default pose and default sprays. Rare recolor skins were gallery-only. The Hero Gallery was per hero, showing owned/total counts per category, and supported equipping or buying with credits.

### Cited Findings
- Common skins are the base appearance, unlocked by default, two per hero (except Echo and post-OW2 heroes). Each hero has four Rare skins that recolor the default. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Skins](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Skins)
- Each hero has one "Heroic" highlight intro by default. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Highlight intros](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Highlight_intros)
- Each hero can equip up to four voice lines via the Hero Gallery and has 1 default "Heroic" voice line. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Voice lines](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Voice_lines)
- Each hero has at least one Common "Default" victory pose and at least two Rare poses. Rare poses cost 75 credits, event poses 200 in OW2 pricing. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Victory poses](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Victory_poses)
- Default player icons: two Overwatch logo versions (Dark/Light) plus "Rainbow". Unlockable icons cost 75 credits. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Player Icons](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Player_Icons)
- Launch cosmetics per hero (2016): 54 total (10 skins, 3 emotes, 3 victory poses, 10 voice lines, 25 sprays, 3 highlight intros). — [Fextralife](https://overwatch.wiki.fextralife.com/Progression_System)
- Achievements each unlock a spray, and are viewable in the Career Profile "Achievements" section. The current wiki list (192 achievements) includes OW2 changes, e.g. the "Level 10/25/50" achievements are now Battle Pass tiers. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Achievements](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Achievements)
- 136 OW1 Competitive Sprays (38 animated) are now unobtainable. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Sprays](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Sprays)
- Hero Gallery (fan wiki, OW2 wording but same UI lineage): select a hero, see all cosmetics, equip owned items, or "Unlock" with credits. Profile (icon) changes are made via Career Profile > Customization in OW2. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Cosmetics](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Cosmetics)
- Seasonal cosmetics carried event icons (Anniversary, Archives, Summer, Halloween, Winter, Lunar). — [Weird Gloop wiki: Cosmetics](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Cosmetics)
- In OW1, golden weapons were under the Hero Gallery "Weapons" tab. In OW2 S8, the tab split into "Weapon Skins" and "Weapon Variants". — [Weird Gloop wiki: Weapon Variants](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Weapon_Variants)
- OW2 legacy-credit prices for OW1 skins: Legendary event 1,500, Legendary 1,000, Epic event 750, Epic 250. This differs from OW1's 3,000 for new event legendaries during the event. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Skins](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Skins)

### Inferences
- The project reports the Hero Gallery shows "0/0" cosmetics. That probably means the client found no unlock records in the account/content provider for each category, so it counts neither owned nor available. The defaults listed above (Classic skins, Heroic intro/voice line, Default pose, default sprays/icons) are the minimum the original servers would have marked owned. Whether defaults are server-granted or client-implied is not established by these sources.
- Categories exposed in OW1 gallery tabs (from the cosmetic types above): Skins, Emotes, Victory Poses, Voice Lines, Sprays, Highlight Intros, Weapons. Player Icons sat outside per-hero tabs.

### Gaps
- Exact per-hero default-unlock lists for the 1.74 build should come from client data (OWLib `ExtractHeroUnlocks` / `ListHeroUnlocks`). No source gives a canonical "default owned" set or the loadout slot schema.
- Loadout slot counts for the final OW1 (e.g., 4 voice lines, 4 sprays on the wheel?, emote wheel) were not fully confirmed. Voice lines = 4 per hero is cited.

## Competitive Play

### Takeaway
Final OW1 Competitive (Season 35, Jul–Sep 2022) unlocked at level 25. It used SR 1–5000 in seven tiers (Bronze–Grandmaster) with a separate Top 500. Role Queue (since Season 18, Sept 2019) had per-role SR and 5 placements per role. Open Queue had its own SR (re-added Season 23, July 2020). Wins paid 15 CP and draws 5 CP. Season-end CP depended on the highest tier per role/queue (e.g., GM 650 per role / 1,750 Open Queue). Golden weapons cost 3,000 CP. Seasons were ~2 months from Season 6 onward. SR decay applied to Diamond+ (25 SR/day after 7 days idle, from Aug 2017).

### Cited Findings
- Season 35 (official forum): Competitive unlocks at level 25. New seasons start "the first Thursday of every other month"; Season 36 was listed for Sept 1, 2022. Five placement matches in a selected role. SR 1–5000, per-role SR in Role Queue plus one Open Queue SR. Tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster. — [Blizzard forums: Welcome to Season 35](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/welcome-to-season-35-of-competitive-play/680081/1)
- Season 35 end-of-season CP by highest tier, as (per role / Open Queue / max total): Bronze 25/65/140; Silver 50/125/275; Gold 100/250/550; Platinum 200/500/1,100; Diamond 300/750/1,650; Master 450/1,200/2,550; Grandmaster 650/1,750/3,700. Completing placements in any role or Open Queue grants a season spray and icon; Top 500 grants an extra icon and an animated spray. — [Blizzard forums: Welcome to Season 35](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/welcome-to-season-35-of-competitive-play/680081/1)
- Top 500 (Season 35): five boards (Tank, Damage, Support, combined average of all three roles, Open Queue). Role boards need ≥25 games in the role, combined needs eligibility on all three, Open Queue needs ≥50 games. — same. Blizzard SMS Protect has been required for Top 500 since June 26, 2018. — [Blizzard patch notes Jun 2018](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2018/06/)
- Per-match CP at the end of OW1: **15 per win, 5 per draw** (fan wiki: OW2 S1–3 kept "the same as the end seasons of Overwatch"). — [Weird Gloop wiki: Competitive Points](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Competitive_Points)
- Pre-role-queue season-end CP (forum quoting old values): Bronze 65, Silver 125, Gold 250, Platinum 500, Diamond 750, Master 1,200, GM 1,750. These became the Open Queue values. — [Blizzard forum thread (search summary)](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/competitive-points-at-the-end-of-the-season/388120)
- SR tier ranges (commonly cited for OW1 7-tier system): Bronze <1,500; Silver 1,500–1,999; Gold 2,000–2,499; Platinum 2,500–2,999; Diamond 3,000–3,499; Master 3,500–3,999; Grandmaster 4,000+. — [esports.gg (search summary)](https://esports.gg/news/overwatch/overwatch-ranks-explained-ranks-in-order-overwatch-sr-top-500-leaderboard-points-and-more/); the fan wiki gives Bronze <1,499 and GM 4,000–4,499 — [search summary of Competitive Play wiki](https://overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Competitive_Play). The OW2 dev comment says decay was "applied to high rank players" each season before OW2, as a display-only "pseudo-Rank decay". — [Weird Gloop wiki: Competitive Play](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Competitive_Play)
- Leaver penalty (general description): leavers could not join another game until the match ended, were not backfilled, took an automatic loss, and received escalating competitive suspensions up to a season ban. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Competitive Play](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Competitive_Play). Nov 2016: skill ratings after placement slightly lower; players distributed more broadly. — [Blizzard patch notes Nov 2016](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2016/11/)
- Competitive Open Queue was also an Arcade mode in parts of 2019–2020 seasons 2–6 of that track. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Competitive Play](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Competitive_Play)
- OW2 changes: SR numbers hidden and replaced by Bronze–Grandmaster plus Champion with 5 divisions, end-of-season CP changed, golden weapons kept at 3,000 CP, decay removed in S4. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Competitive Points](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Competitive_Points)

### Inferences
- Minimal server state per player per season: `{role: tank/damage/support/open → SR (int 1..5000) | null, placement_games_played, placement_wins, games_played, wins, losses, draws, highest_tier_this_season}`, plus a lifetime CP balance and per-season "season high" for end-of-season grants.
- Season ID 35 (or 36) is the natural "current season" for a preserved 1.74.0.0.104319 server. Build 104319 is from mid-2022. Which season number the client expects is a client/server-data question to verify in the lobby data.

### Gaps
- Exact per-match SR gain/loss formula and placement-SR caps (e.g., placements capped at prior season's SR) were not found from primary sources.
- Group SR restrictions (commonly 1,000 SR, 500 SR for Diamond+/GM) were not confirmed from an accessible source.
- Exact leaver SR penalties and suspension durations for OW1 were not found.
- Whether SR decay still existed in 2022 OW1 is uncertain (the Kaplan "no more decay" statement is undated in the snippet).
- The exact per-win CP history (1 per win in early seasons, changed to 10 and later 15) could not be dated.

## Career profile, stats, achievements, medals

### Takeaway
The OW1 career profile showed level/prestige/border, endorsement level and category breakdown, per-role competitive SR/tier, and Quick Play/Competitive stats per hero and overall. Stat categories were Combat, Assists, Best, Average, Game, Miscellaneous, Match Awards and Hero Specific. Achievements unlocked sprays. Profiles defaulted to Friends Only from June 26, 2018, with privacy settings. Medals were gold/silver/bronze per stat in each match and were removed in OW2.

### Cited Findings
- Career profile data categories (scraped from the OW1-era public career pages): modes `quickplay` and `competitive`. Each hero and `overall` has `combat`, `assists`, `best`, `average`, `game`, `miscellaneous` and `match_awards`. Profile fields: battletag, `rank` per role (`tank`/`damage`/`support` with `sr`, role/tier icons), `level`, `prestige`, `endorsementLevel`, `endorsements` {shotcaller, teammate, sportsmanship}, icon/border/stars URLs, and most-played time per hero. — [overwatch-stats-api README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/overwatch-stats-api@2.1.0/README.md)
- Career Profiles became non-public by default: "Career Profiles will no longer be public by default (now defaults to Friends Only)" (June 26, 2018). — [Blizzard patch notes Jun 2018](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2018/06/)
- Medals: given per stat relative to the lobby (gold best, silver second, bronze third). Removed in OW2. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Medals](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Medals). Medals also gave XP (bronze 50 / silver 100 / gold 150, highest only). — [Fextralife](https://overwatch.wiki.fextralife.com/Progression_System)
- Achievements unlock a spray each and are shown under Career Profile → Achievements. Examples (OW1-era general achievements): "Centenary" (win 100 QP/Competitive games), "Decked Out" (50 unlocks for one hero), "Survival Expert" (heal 900 with health packs in one life), "The Friend Zone" (play in a group with a friend), "The Path Is Closed" (destroy 3 Symmetra teleporters), "Undying" (20-kill streak). The original OW1 level achievements were Level 10/25/50 by player level (now Battle Pass tiers). — [Weird Gloop wiki: Achievements](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Achievements)

### Inferences
- A preservation server needs a stat store keyed by `(mode, hero_or_overall, stat_id)`, with stats grouped by the client's own stat-category GUIDs. The 1.74 client data (OWLib) should hold the authoritative stat list, and the "25800 family stats" lobby messages likely carry these records. Unverified against this build.

### Gaps
- The full per-hero stat list and the exact OW1 achievement list (OW1 had fewer than the current 192) should be dumped from client data (OWLib `ListAchievements`). No web source gives the 2022 OW1 list exactly.
- The privacy options in 2022 (Public / Friends Only / Private) are not quoted from a primary source here, only the 2018 default change.
- "Cards" (end-of-match commendation cards / voting) mechanics were not researched due to tool budget.

## Social: groups, LFG, endorsements, chat, reporting

### Takeaway
Endorsements (June 26, 2018) let players endorse in three categories: Sportsmanship (any player), Shot Caller and Good Teammate (teammates only). Endorsement level runs 1–5, decays per match and drops to 0 on penalties. In OW1 the reward was periodic Loot Boxes. Looking For Group (June 2018) let leaders set a name, mode, voice preference, minimum endorsement level and per-slot roles. It was removed in OW2. Silenced players lost team/match/general chat but kept group chat and whispers to friends.

### Cited Findings
- June 26, 2018 patch: endorsements "Recognize commendable behavior for players exhibiting sportsmanship, being a good teammate, or for shot calling". "Those who consistently maintain a high endorsement level will receive periodic rewards"; those with negative behavior or suspensions "will lose their endorsements". Endorsements are visible on Career Profiles and the Groups menu. — [Blizzard patch notes Jun 2018](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2018/06/)
- Pre-OW2 categories: Shot Caller and Good Teammate for teammates only, Sportsmanship for anyone. The level icon showed the ratio of categories received, and the rewards were Loot Boxes. Rules (current wiki): up to 2 endorsements per game, same player once per 12 h, none in custom games. Levels 1–5, everyone starts at 1. Level decays slightly after every match, with less decay when lobby members recently endorsed you. Reporting penalties set level 0. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Endorsements](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Endorsements)
- Aug 13, 2019: endorsements extended to Mystery Heroes, No Limits, Deathmatch, Total Mayhem, Low Grav and all PvE event modes. — [Blizzard patch notes Aug 2019](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2019/08/)
- Looking For Group (June 26, 2018). Options: group name; mode (Quick Play, Competitive [with SR range], Any, Custom Game, VS. AI); voice chat wanted; minimum endorsement level; per-slot role (Any/Tank/Damage/Support/Disabled) with "Enforce Roles" and secondary roles. Removed in OW2. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Looking for Group](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Looking_for_Group); [Blizzard patch notes Jun 2018](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2018/06/) ("These specifications will then be activated in-game")
- Silence penalty: players cannot use Team chat, Private Game channels, General, Match channels, or whisper/reply to non-friends. They can use Group chat and whisper friends. Only unique reporters count. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Penalties](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Penalties)
- Report categories (OW2-era): Inappropriate communication (text/voice/spam), Inappropriate name, Cheating (hacking, boosting/deranking), Gameplay sabotage (AFK, feeding, blocking progress, griefing), Emergency. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Penalties](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Penalties)
- Leaver status: repeat leavers enter "leaver status" until they complete enough games, shown on the player card. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Penalties](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Penalties)

### Inferences
- Chat channels evident from the silence table: Team, Match, Group, General, Private Game (custom) and whispers. Friends/presence come from Battle.net (BGS), not the OW lobby. The local server must emulate the BGS friends/presence services for the Social menu.
- Endorsement record to serve: `{level 0..5, shotcaller_ratio, teammate_ratio, sportsmanship_ratio}` (profile scraping shows a 3-way breakdown).

### Gaps
- Exact endorsement-level formula, decay rate and thresholds were never published.
- Max group size (6 in QP/Comp) and competitive group SR limits are not cited from a primary source.
- OW1 chat-channel level requirements (OW2 requires endorsement 2 for team/match chat) are not established for OW1.

## Seasonal events and timing

### Takeaway
OW1 ran six annual events, each three weeks long (Archives and Anniversary started 2017). Each event brought themed brawls, decorated maps, event Loot Boxes with exclusive items (new items at 3× credits) and later weekly challenges. In 2022 the Anniversary Remix volumes replaced Archives and Summer Games. Loot box sales ended Aug 30, 2022.

### Cited Findings
- Summer Games (brawl: Lúcioball): 2016 Aug 2–23; 2017 Aug 8–29; 2018 Aug 9–30; 2019 Jul 16–Aug 5; 2020 Aug 4–25; 2021 Jul 20–Aug 10; no 2022 OW1 Summer Games. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Summer Games](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Summer_Games); [Seasonal events](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Seasonal_events)
- Halloween Terror (Junkenstein's Revenge, Eichenwalde/Hollywood decorated): 2016 Oct 11–Nov 1; 2017 Oct 10–Nov 1; 2018 Oct 9–31; 2019 Oct 15–Nov 4; 2020 Oct 13–Nov 3; 2021 Oct 12–Nov 2 (2022 was OW2). — [Weird Gloop wiki: Halloween Terror](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Halloween_Terror)
- Winter Wonderland (Mei's Snowball Offensive, Yeti Hunt; King's Row/Hanamura/Blizzard World decorated): 2016 Dec 13–Jan 2; 2017 Dec 12–Jan 1; 2018 Dec 11–Jan 2; 2019 Dec 10–Jan 2; 2020 Dec 15–Jan 5; 2021 Dec 16–Jan 6. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Winter Wonderland](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Winter_Wonderland)
- Lunar New Year (Capture the Flag; Lijiang decorated; Ayutthaya added 2018): 2017 Jan 24–Feb 13 (Rooster); 2018 (Dog) listed as "January 8 to March 5" (likely a wiki typo for Feb 8); 2019 Jan 24–Feb 18 (Pig); 2020 Jan 16–Feb 5 (Rat); 2021 Feb 4–25 (Ox); 2022 Jan 25–Feb 15 (Tiger; brawls Bounty Hunter and CTF Blitz). — [Weird Gloop wiki: Lunar New Year](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Lunar_New_Year); [Game Informer 2022](https://gameinformer.com/2022/01/25/overwatchs-2022-lunar-new-year-event-kicks-off-today)
- Archives (PvE: Uprising 2017, Retribution 2018, Storm Rising 2019; all three plus challenge modifiers in 2020/2021): 2017 Apr 11–May 1; 2018 Apr 10–30; 2019 Apr 16–May 6; 2020 Mar 12–Apr 2; 2021 Apr 6–27. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Overwatch Archives](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Overwatch_Archives)
- Anniversary: 2017 May 23–Jun 12; 2018 May 22–Jun 11; 2019 May 21–Jun 10; 2020 May 19–Jun 9; 2021 May 18–Jun 8; 2022 May 17–Jun 7. In OW1, Anniversary let players unlock any past event skin. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Overwatch Anniversary](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Overwatch_Anniversary)
- 2022 Anniversary Remix Vol. 3 (Aug 9–30, 2022): daily rotating brawls (Archives Aug 9/16/23/30; Archives Challenge Aug 10/17/24; Summer Games Aug 11/18/25; Halloween Aug 12/19/26; Halloween Challenge Aug 13/20/27; Winter Aug 14/21/28; Lunar New Year Aug 15/22/29). — [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/overwatch-anniversary-now-live-loot-boxes-ending-on-august-30/amp/); [Blizzard Watch](https://blizzardwatch.com/2022/08/09/overwatch-summer-games-2022/)
- Events add a custom Arcade brawl and limited-time cosmetics via event challenges. — [Weird Gloop wiki: Seasonal events](https://overwatch.weirdgloop.org/w/Seasonal_events)

### Inferences
- In the client, events are probably toggled by server-supplied configuration (active event ID / hotfix data affecting the main-menu theme, Arcade brawl list and loot-box type). The project's lobby 20505 "hotfix" and 20504 "key" lists are candidates for this. Unverified; no web source describes the wire mechanism.
- For a preservation server, a "no event" default is safest. A config table keyed by the dates above could replay events.

### Gaps
- How the original servers flagged an active event to the client (config record names/IDs) is not documented publicly. It must come from client RE.
- Event weekly-challenge reward specifics per year (e.g., 9/18/27 wins for icon/spray/skin) were not collected due to tool budget.
